## B3 — blocking
Claim: The ten-line ceiling's stated exclusion — "the hook's own frontmatter flip … is four of those lines on every revision" — is wrong for grandfathered documents, where it is two, over-allowing the ceiling by 20%.
Location: `policies/document-metadata-policy.md:147-153` (condition 2), `docs/packages/package-d-change-package.md:139-143` (§5)
Evidence: **Verified by running,** in a scratch clone at `4176b6b` with the hook installed. Case A, an ordinary `agreed` document (`last-reviewed` non-null): a 2-line body insertion committed to `README.md` printed `FLIPPED README.md: agreed -> in-review` and produced `git diff --numstat` = `4 2` — six changed lines, four of them the flip. The stated constant holds. Case B, the same document made `agreed` with `last-reviewed: null` — the exact shape the grandfather clause at `policies/document-metadata-policy.md:85-92` produces: a 1-line body insertion produced `2 1` — three changed lines, of which the flip contributed **two**, not four, because `last-reviewed` was already `null` and the flip rewrote only the `status:` line.
Consequence: An author following the stated arithmetic subtracts 4 from `numstat` on a grandfathered document and computes a body count two lines under the truth, so a twelve-line edit measures as ten and passes a ten-line ceiling. The grandfather clause is precisely the mechanism by which adopting repos arrive at `agreed` with `last-reviewed: null` (`:85-92` — *"at adoption, the adopting repo records a one-time per-document disposition list"*), so the wrong case is the common case in exactly the repos this policy is written to travel to. This defect was introduced by the post-triage fix, not by the directive — see N2.
Fix: Drop the constant. The quantity condition 2 wants is *changed lines of document body*, which is what `git diff --numstat` gives once the frontmatter block is excluded from the diff, and which needs no arithmetic and no case analysis. If a constant is kept, it has to be stated as "two or four depending on whether `last-reviewed` was already null," which is an argument for dropping it.
Related: B2
