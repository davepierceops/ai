## B1 — blocking

## N1 — non-blocking
Claim: The schema governs artifacts in `reviews/` and states no filename convention for them, though `last-reviewed:` points at those filenames and `bin/flip-agreed` validates the pointer.
Location: `skills/spec-review-cycle.md:132-138` and `:176-206`
Evidence: Verified by running. `:134` reads "Review artifacts live in `reviews/` and are what `last-reviewed:` points at"; the Header block (`:181-192`) specifies the artifact's *content* and no path. `git show 81bd2de:policies/document-metadata-policy.md` specifies the pointer's format (`<reviews/path.md> @ <sha>`) and no naming rule for the target. `bin/cycle-open --help` writes the *directive* skeleton and takes `--out DIR` for the upload bundle; it does not name review artifacts. The convention is real but unwritten: `git ls-tree --name-only 81bd2de reviews/` shows `<doc-stem>-cycle-<n>.md` throughout. The evidence that it is unwritten is that `docs/cycles/trivium-gate-2026-08-07-directive.md` had to dictate it (`reviews/<doc-stem>-cycle-1.md`) rather than cite it.
Consequence: The one convention that makes `last-reviewed` pointers predictable is carried by imitation, and each directive has to restate it or risk an executor inventing a name. The stem rule also collides with itself on this document: `<doc-stem>-cycle-<n>` for `spec-review-cycle.md` yields `spec-review-cycle-cycle-1.md`, which this artifact is named, and a reader cannot tell from the name whether "cycle-1" is the cycle number or part of the stem. A future document whose stem ends in a number would be worse.
Fix: State the convention where the schema is stated — `reviews/<document stem>-cycle-<n>.md`, stem taken from the document's basename without extension — and say what to do when the stem already ends in `-cycle` or a digit. One sentence in `### What this schema governs`.

## N2 — non-blocking
Claim: Fourth-use feedback — the schema still has no shape for a check that was run and passed, and this review had to invent an unschema'd section in each of five artifacts to record one.
Location: `skills/spec-review-cycle.md:208-233`
Evidence: Verified by reading, and by direct use. The three entry severities are `blocking`, `non-blocking`, `observation`; every entry requires `Consequence:` ("what goes wrong, concretely"), and `:230-231` states that an entry that cannot fill it is an observation — but a passed check has no consequence to state either, so it fits no bucket. `OPEN-ITEMS.md:547-585` records this as item 3 of third-use feedback from the cycle-5 gate review, verbatim: "the schema has no shape for a check that passed … the artifact carries `Consequence: None` four times. Without those entries a reader cannot distinguish 'the check passed' from 'the check was never run', which is the distinction `Not inspected` exists to protect." That item is open and records "Not acted on in Package D." This review reproduced the friction independently in all five artifacts — e.g. verifying by running that `skills/spec-review-cycle.md:84-97`'s "enforced by tool since 2026-08-02" is exactly right (`git log -S "enforced by tool since"` → `b1773d0`, 2026-08-02; `git log -- bin/flip-agreed` → `4e90b03`, 2026-08-02, "enforce that a cited SHA is named by an entry in the expedited log"), a load-bearing check with nowhere in the schema to land.
Consequence: A reader of any of these five artifacts cannot distinguish, from the schema's fields alone, a claim that was checked and held from one that was never checked — the distinction `Not inspected` exists to protect, defeated from the other side. In practice reviewers either omit passed checks (losing the evidence that the review was thorough where it matters most, on the claims that turned out fine) or stretch `Scope` and `observation` to hold them, which is what happened here.
Fix: Add a fourth entry severity or a header field for verified-and-held checks, with `Consequence` not required — or state explicitly that `Scope` is where they go and that it may run long. `OPEN-ITEMS.md:547` items 1, 2, and 4 are the same feedback round and are unaddressed; this is the natural cycle to take all four.
