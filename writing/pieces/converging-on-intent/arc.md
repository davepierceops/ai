# Arc

A personal contradiction — decades of "I'm not a coder, I don't enjoy
coding" alongside genuine enjoyment of making systems do things — resolves
into a mechanism: coding was translation, translation is lossy, and LLMs
move the lossy boundary rather than removing it. The hot take: the old
process was never clean — intent → implementation was always lossy, and
production was always emergent and practically nondeterministic. SRE has
been asking the resulting epistemic question (what is the system actually
doing, and what evidence supports that belief?) for decades on the
operations side. The piece turns that question on construction and ends
open: what evidence do we have that the thing we're building is converging
on what we intended?
