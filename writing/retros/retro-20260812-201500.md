# Retro — writing — 2026-08-12

## Context

Bootstrapped a public-prose methodology from scratch: decided criteria
dimensions one question at a time, drafted the governing criteria doc and five
roles, created `davepierceops/writing` (private), and landed everything
through operator-run command blocks after MCP proved unable to access the
repo.

## Evidence

1. Dave rejected criteria-extraction from his old instruction sets ("I think
   I know more about what I'm trying to do now") in favor of
   question-driven, blank-slate criteria.
2. The question sequence stacked questions at least three times; Dave caught
   it ("How many questions was that").
3. The claims taxonomy (relayed / demonstrated / inferred / opinion) came
   out of Dave's own four-category framing, unprompted by the evidence
   vocabulary.
4. "load-bearing" was named as a Claude tell while the drafts themselves
   used it — including in the criteria being drafted.
5. Cross-repo leakage: eight conversation-context references (CoS analogies,
   "Quadrivium," unnamed "the methodology") were found in the first two
   drafts on a single directed scan.
6. Prose-in-repo was caught by Dave as derived-artifact drift ("isn't that
   the point where the deliverable begins to live in my google doc?");
   resolved to repo-never-holds-prose.
7. MCP could not create the repo (403) nor read it after creation (404);
   the session pivoted to operator-owned writes via command blocks, then
   adopted that as the standing mode by choice.
8. The synchronous per-line voice-approval design was rejected for flow
   cost; walking the actual flow end-to-end produced the inbox design and
   reversed Dave's initial objection to end-of-session harvest.
9. `bin/session-tar` gained a dirty-tree refusal after one question about
   SHA-describability.

## Interpretation

- (2) One-question-at-a-time degrades under momentum; the stacking was
  caught by Dave each time, not self-caught.
- (4, 5) Drafting instruction docs in a session saturated with prose-voice
  discussion measurably contaminates them — the Instruction Reviewer role
  exists because of this observation, and its first run should be over
  everything drafted today.
- (6) Derived-artifact drift instincts from the engineering methodology
  transfer directly to prose workflows.
- (7) Operator-owned repos with agent-emitted command blocks are a viable
  standing mode, not merely a fallback — verification moves to the operator,
  matching the Track B stance.
- (8) Walking a concrete flow end-to-end resolves design questions that
  abstract discussion deadlocks; Dave reversed himself only after narrating
  the actual sequence of pastes.

## Durable insights

- Bootstrap criteria by interrogation, one dimension per question, when the
  author's understanding has outgrown their prior written artifacts.
- Pair every frictionless-capture mechanism with a scheduled pruning
  counterweight (voice inbox ↔ Instruction Reviewer over the criteria doc).
- Scan freshly drafted documents for conversation-context leakage as a named
  pass before landing them in a repo the conversation cannot travel to.

## Candidate methodology changes

- None for `davepierceops/ai` from this session. The borrowed-term pattern
  (bundle, baton adopted with lighter local meanings; directive declined)
  may eventually warrant a LEXICON note on cross-repo term borrowing —
  raise only if it recurs.
